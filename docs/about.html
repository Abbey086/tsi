Our Story

Founded on purpose,
built for growth.

Talent Search Initiative (TSI) was founded on September 3, 2025, in Kampala, Uganda, with a burning desire to bridge the gap between raw potential and professional success.

We believe that every young person possesses a unique gift that, if properly nurtured, can become a vehicle for both personal success and national development.

Future outlook

Our Vision

To build a Uganda where every talent is discovered, nurtured and transformed into a vehicle for personal and national development.

Core purpose

Our Mission

To discover, empower and promote talents across all sectors, fostering creativity, innovation and sustainable livelihoods for young people and community.

Guiding Pillars

Our Core
Values.

Principles that define our culture, operations, and commitment to every emerging talent we support.

Integrity

We uphold the highest standards of honesty and transparency.

Inclusivity

We welcome talents from all backgrounds, regions, and communities.

Creativity

We celebrate original thinking, artistic expression, and innovation.

Commitment

We are dedicated to long-term impact and individual youth success.

Transparency

We operate with complete openness in all our leadership and initiatives.

Innovation

We embrace new methods and forward-thinking pathways to solve problems.

What driving us

Our Strategic
Objectives.

01 / IDENTIFY

Discover Talent

Identify and nurture talents across music, arts, sports, technology, agriculture, and entrepreneurship.

02 / DEVELOP

Mentorship & Training

Provide comprehensive mentorship, structured skills training, and capacity-building development programs.

03 / SHOWCASE

Expos & Competitions

Organize talent shows, competitions, exhibitions, and platforms for public recognition.

04 / PARTNER

Collaboration

Collaborate with government agencies, private sector leaders, and civil society organizations.

05 / SUSTAIN

Commercialization

Support talent commercialization, business incubation, and self-employment pathways.

06 / ADVOCATE

Policy Support

Advocate for policies supporting sustainable youth talent development and creative sector funding.
