Who we are

We help talent
become visible.

Founded in Kampala, Uganda, Talent Search Initiative is committed to discovering, empowering and promoting young people across creative, communication and physical disciplines. We connect potential with mentorship, professional development and real platforms for visibility.

500+ Talents discovered
50+ Programs and development pathways
20+ Partners and collaborators
01 / DISCOVER

Find the signal.

School searches, community outreach and targeted talent identification uncover exceptional ability that deserves to be developed and seen.

02 / NURTURE

Build the craft.

Training, mentorship, workshops and practical guidance turn raw potential into confidence, skill and professional readiness.

03 / SHOWCASE

Create opportunity.

We create platforms where talent can be presented professionally, gain visibility and take the next step toward sustainable opportunity.

What we spot

More than one
kind of talent.

TSI supports creative, communication and physical disciplines. The goal is not simply to identify a skill, but to develop the person behind it and build a credible path toward opportunity.

Modelling & Visual

Modelling, fashion presentation, visual storytelling, image and personal brand development.

Communication

Public speaking, debate, poetry, writing and other disciplines built around expression.

Physical Talent

Sports, athletics and team disciplines where training, performance and discipline matter.
